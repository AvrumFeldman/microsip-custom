MicroSIP Custom 3.22.16.3 - Windows voice calls
============================================

This is an independently maintained fork, not an official MicroSIP release.
Download/update: https://github.com/AvrumFeldman/microsip-custom/releases
Source and public PRs: https://github.com/AvrumFeldman/microsip-custom
PRs are welcome and appreciated, including AI-assisted and AI-generated ones.

Start
-----
Extract to a separate writable folder and run microsip.exe. Keep
MicroSIPAudioGuard.exe beside it. Close any other MicroSIP instance first.
Add your SIP account and re-enter its password. Official saved passwords
cannot be imported because the public source omits the official encryption
key. Your original installation is not replaced. Contacts can be exported
from your original copy and imported here.

Audio changes
-------------
Entering digits and incoming ringtones use playback only. Incoming early
media cannot activate capture before you answer. Incoming ringing alone
does not grab capture or mute music. Capture is released after the final call ends, even
before a remote SIP hangup acknowledgment. Periodic state reconciliation
cleans up stale microphone ownership.

Menu > Settings > Other audio during calls offers:
  Leave other audio unchanged (default)
  Mute all other apps
  Mute selected apps
For selected apps use names such as Spotify.exe; vlc.exe, or Add app...
Matching is case-insensitive. A browser name affects the whole browser.

Muting begins when dialing an outgoing call or answering an incoming call,
continues through hold/overlapping calls, and ends after the final call.
Apps already muted stay muted; volume levels are unchanged. A manual unmute
during the call takes precedence for that session until the next call.
New audio sessions are detected every 200 ms. MicroSIP and system sounds
are excluded. The audio guard restores music if MicroSIP crashes/exits;
it cannot restore if the guard itself is killed or Windows audio fails.
Exclusive-mode players may not follow Windows session mute controls.

Calls
-----
CONF > Add participant / Remove participant works in single-call mode.
Choose the participant to remove; only that conference leg is disconnected.

Transfer's dropdown offers Blind Transfer and Attended Transfer (consult
first). For attended transfer enter the destination and confirm, speak to
the destination, then select Complete attended transfer. Cancel consultation
and return resumes the original held call. End also cancels a consultation,
even while ringing. Calls routed back to the same account have separate
Answer/Decline controls and preserve the original held call when answered.
PBX/provider support for SIP
REFER with Replaces is required. Existing PBX feature-code settings remain
available.

A forced process termination cannot send SIP hangup messages. The remote
call may remain until the other party hangs up or the PBX detects the lost
session. The audio guard restores Windows audio, not SIP calls.

Build differences and validation
--------------------------------
The dialer hides messaging and video quick buttons for this calls-only
build, while retaining their source code. It uses a full-width Call button
and keeps the number field near the tabs instead of adding blank top space.
Windows taskbar identity and icon fallbacks are explicitly set.
Name/number separators in the separate call window display correctly.

Based on published MicroSIP 3.22.16 source, not the newer official 3.22.18
binary. Voice only; WAV recording; PJSIP audio codecs plus Opus. No video,
official MP3 recorder, or external G.729/AMR/SILK/G.722.1 codecs. TLS and
SRTP/DTLS are included. The executables are unsigned.
PJSIP includes public MP3 writer source, but its stock recorder does not
wire that writer to an encoder. MP3 needs integration and recording tests;
WAV-only is this custom build's limitation, not a licensing requirement.

XML/regex helpers with restrictive bundled-source notices were replaced;
an unused message-box helper was removed. Original upstream authorship
and applicable license notices are preserved.

Regression tests use synthetic local SIP calls, silent Windows render
sessions and account-free test data. Real headset Bluetooth switching and
provider/PBX transfer compatibility still require a check on your setup.
See README.md for build commands, test prerequisites and detailed behavior.
